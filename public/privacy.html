Privacy Policy

Bollywood Bedtime · Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your children's names and ages stay on your device. If you ask the app to write a new story, only the film's title, the age range and the story language are sent to our server to write it.

Who this app is for

Bollywood Bedtime is made for parents and carers to use with their children. A parent sets the app up and chooses the stories. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your children's first names (optional), age ranges and story language
  • Which stories were played, used to suggest a different story each night
  • Settings such as how many stories to play per night
  • Stories you've made with "Make a story from any film"

This is stored only in the app's storage on your phone or in your browser. We never receive it. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

Information sent when you make a new story

When you use "Make a story from any film", the app sends three things to our server: the film title you typed, the age range, and the story language. Your child's name is never sent. Our server passes the request to Anthropic, the AI company whose Claude model writes the story, and returns the story to your device. We don't store the request or the story on our server.

Like any website, our hosting provider (Netlify) briefly processes your IP address to deliver the request. We use it only to slow down unusually rapid repeated requests, and we don't keep it.

Film posters

For stories you make yourself, the app looks up the film's poster from The Movie Database (TMDB) by sending the film title. TMDB receives your device's IP address as part of that request. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Narration

Bundled stories are pre-recorded and play from inside the app. Other stories are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech voice, on your device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts or location
  • We never sell or share personal information

About the stories

Stories are gentle, child-friendly retellings inspired by much-loved Indian films. Bollywood Bedtime isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the films, their makers or their studios.

Changes

If this app starts collecting anything new, we'll update this page before it does, and change the date at the top.
